# 🌊 CaliForm – AI-Powered Calisthenics Coach

> Real-Time Biomechanics Analysis for Calisthenics Athletes using Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 📖 Overview

**CaliForm** is an AI-powered mobile application designed specifically for Calisthenics and Street Workout athletes.

Unlike traditional fitness apps that focus on gym-based exercises and simple repetition counting, CaliForm acts as a real-time digital coach capable of analyzing complex body mechanics, joint angles, and leverage positions directly through a smartphone camera.

Using advanced pose estimation and biomechanical analysis, CaliForm provides instant audio feedback during exercise execution, helping athletes improve technique, reduce injury risk, and accelerate skill progression.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Calisthenics is one of the fastest-growing fitness disciplines worldwide, but it comes with a steep technical learning curve.

Advanced skills such as:

- Handstand
- Muscle-Up
- Front Lever
- Back Lever
- Planche
- Human Flag

require precise body positioning, scapular control, and joint alignment.

Most athletes currently rely on:

- Recording themselves
- Reviewing videos afterward
- Guessing technical mistakes
- Expensive personal coaching

This process is inefficient, slow, and often leads to injuries caused by poor technique and overuse.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CaliForm transforms a smartphone into a personal AI Calisthenics Coach.

The user simply places their phone several meters away and starts training.

The app:

1. Detects the athlete's body using computer vision
2. Creates a real-time digital skeleton overlay
3. Measures body alignment and joint angles
4. Compares movement patterns against biomechanical reference models
5. Provides immediate audio corrections while the exercise is being performed

**Example feedback:**

- *"Raise your hips."*
- *"Lock your elbows."*
- *"Retract your shoulder blades."*
- *"Keep your body straight."*

The athlete receives coaching in real time — instead of after the workout.
